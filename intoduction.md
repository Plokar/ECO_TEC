Pracuji na fiktivnim startupu co budou mit aplikaci jako BeReal ale pro sbirani odpodku kde budou dostavat challenge a muzes competitiovat s kamarady kdo zachranuje planetu vice. Hodne gamifimace adiktivnes atd... A potřebuji vyplnit tyto papíry a vymyslet tomu nejake dalsi funkce co by byli zajmave a davali by nam nejakou market vyhodu

Jo — ten koncept má podle mě **mnohem větší potenciál než jen „BeReal, ale sbíráš odpad“**. Klíčová věc, která vám může dát market advantage, je udělat z toho **social game + real-world impact platform**, kde se každý skutečný ekologický čin promění v herní progress.

Podle papírů bych to postavil jako:

* **Company:** EcoTech
* **App:** EcoQuest
* **Headquarters:** Netherlands
* **Core idea:** *Turn real-world environmental actions into a social game.*

## 1. Description of the domain

| Question                        | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **How did this domain evolve?** | Environmental awareness has moved from traditional recycling campaigns toward digital platforms, social media, gamification and community-based challenges. Young people increasingly interact with environmental topics through apps and social networks. |
| **Future tendencies?**          | More gamification, AI verification, personalized challenges, local environmental communities, reward systems and cooperation with municipalities and companies.                                                                                            |
| **Possible risks?**             | Fake activity, greenwashing, low user retention, privacy concerns, competition from large social-media platforms and users losing interest after the initial novelty.                                                                                      |
| **Important companies?**        | Ecosia, Too Good To Go, Strava, BeReal and various environmental/recycling platforms. Our advantage is combining their concepts rather than focusing on only one activity.                                                                                 |
| **Specific expenses?**          | App development, servers, AI/image recognition, marketing, employees, legal costs, cybersecurity and partnerships/rewards.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Profit rate?**                | Initially low or negative because of development and marketing costs. The business could become highly scalable because the main product is digital.                                                                                                       |
| **Risk of failure?**            | Medium/high during the first years because social apps depend heavily on user growth and retention.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Number of companies?**        | There are many sustainability apps, but relatively few combine environmental actions, social competition and real-world verification.                                                                                                                      |
| **Small or big companies?**     | The market contains both startups and large companies. Large social platforms could become competitors if they introduce similar features.                                                                                                                 |

# 2. What exactly is EcoQuest?

I would make the **core loop** extremely simple:

**Challenge → Do something in the real world → Take proof → AI verifies it → Earn XP → Compete → Unlock rewards → Get another challenge**

For example:

> 🌱 **TODAY'S QUEST**
> "Collect 10 pieces of plastic."
>
> 📸 Take a photo
> 🤖 AI verifies it
> +150 XP
> +25 EcoPoints
> 🔥 Streak maintained
>
> **You are #3 in your city this week.**

That is much more compelling than just a recycling tracker.

# 3. Features that could give you a real market advantage

### 🤖 1. AI Eco Verification

This could be your **biggest technological advantage**.

User takes a photo and AI checks:

* Is there actually litter?
* How many objects?
* What type of waste?
* Does the photo match the challenge?
* Is the photo potentially recycled/fake?
* Is the location plausible?

You could combine:

**AI image recognition + GPS + timestamp + optional before/after photos**

This makes cheating much harder.

---

### 📍 2. Real-world Eco Map

Instead of just showing a leaderboard, create a map.

Users can see:

* 🗑️ areas with lots of litter
* ♻️ recycling points
* 🌳 areas needing cleanup
* 🌱 completed challenges
* 👥 community cleanups
* 🏆 local environmental hotspots

Eventually municipalities could use the aggregated anonymous data to understand where environmental problems are.

**That creates a potential B2B product.**

---

### ⚔️ 3. Friends vs Friends

This is where the BeReal inspiration becomes powerful.

For example:

**Seb vs Alex**

|                   |    Seb |   Alex |
| ----------------- | -----: | -----: |
| Plastic collected |     47 |     31 |
| Trees planted     |      2 |      5 |
| CO₂ saved         | 8.2 kg | 6.7 kg |
| XP                |  4,820 |  4,350 |

Then:

> **🔥 Alex challenged you to beat his score this week.**

Now you're not competing against an abstract environmental goal.

**You're competing against your friends.**

---

### 🏙️ 4. City vs City

This could become extremely viral.

For example:

> 🇨🇿 **PRAGUE vs AMSTERDAM**
>
> Prague: 18,420 EcoPoints
> Amsterdam: 17,930 EcoPoints
>
> **PRAGUE IS WINNING 🏆**

Schools, universities and youth organizations could create their own teams.

---

### 🏫 5. School Mode

This is potentially a **very strong distribution channel**.

A school creates:

> **SPŠE Plzeň EcoLeague**

Students compete for points.

Classes compete against each other.

```text
2.P       14,820 XP
3.A       12,420 XP
1.B       10,230 XP
```

The school gets statistics and can organize real-world cleanup events.

This gives you a way to acquire **hundreds of users at once**, rather than trying to convince people individually.

---

### 🏆 6. Seasons

Instead of infinite leaderboards:

**EcoQuest Season 01 — Summer 2026**

Every 30 days:

* rankings reset
* new challenges
* new badges
* seasonal rewards
* special events

This gives users a reason to come back.

---

### 🔥 7. Streaks

But I wouldn't make the app *only* about streaks.

Have several:

**Daily streak:**
Complete one eco action every day.

**Friend streak:**
You and your friend complete challenges together.

**Community streak:**
Your city collectively completes challenges for 30 days.

That creates social pressure without making the whole app dependent on one number.

---

### 🎁 8. Real-world rewards

EcoPoints could be exchanged for things like:

* discounts at sustainable businesses
* public transport rewards
* sustainable products
* event tickets
* coffee
* donations to environmental projects

And companies could **sponsor challenges**.

Example:

> 🌲 **Plant 5 trees**
>
> Sponsored by **X**
>
> Reward: 500 EcoPoints

This creates one of your revenue streams.

# 4. One particularly interesting feature: Eco Quests

Don't only generate generic challenges.

Make challenges **location-aware**.

For example:

You're walking through Plzeň.

The app detects you're near a park.

> 📍 **NEW QUEST**
>
> There is a cleanup event 400m away.
>
> **Clean 5 pieces of litter**
>
> Reward: **300 XP**

Or:

> 🚲 You're 2 km from your destination.
>
> **Cycle instead of taking a car**
>
> +250 XP

Now EcoQuest isn't just a social network.

It's a **game layered on top of real life.**

---

# 5. Another strong differentiator: Eco Level

Don't only measure "how much rubbish you collected."

Create a broader **EcoScore**.

For example:

**Your EcoScore: 742**

Based on:

* recycling
* litter collection
* public transport
* cycling/walking
* reusable products
* community activities
* volunteering

But importantly, **don't let users simply claim actions**. The more important actions should require some kind of verification.

# 7. Financing

Your current idea:

> **FREE → ADS**

is okay, but **I wouldn't make advertising your main business model**.

That's too weak for a startup like this.

I'd use a combination:

### 🆓 Free

* basic challenges
* friends
* leaderboard
* basic profile
* advertisements

### ⭐ EcoQuest+

Maybe €3.99/month:

* no ads
* advanced statistics
* exclusive challenges
* custom challenges
* advanced profile
* additional customization

### 🏢 B2B

Companies pay to:

* sponsor challenges
* create branded environmental campaigns
* reward users
* access aggregated environmental statistics

### 🏙️ Municipalities

Cities could pay for:

**EcoQuest City Dashboard**

They could see anonymized statistics about:

* community cleanups
* reported litter
* participation
* environmental campaigns

### 🎁 Partnerships

Companies pay to offer rewards to EcoQuest users.

This gives you **four revenue streams instead of relying entirely on ads.**

# 8. Your biggest competitive advantage

If I were pitching this, I wouldn't say:

> **"It's BeReal for recycling."**

That's catchy, but it makes the idea sound like a copy.

I'd say:

> **"EcoQuest turns environmental action into a social game."**

And your differentiation would be:

**BeReal** → social interaction
**Strava** → competition + real-world activity
**Duolingo** → streaks + gamification
**Too Good To Go** → environmental action
**EcoQuest** → **all of them combined around real-world environmental impact**

That's a much stronger positioning.
